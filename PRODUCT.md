# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations staff who manage CCN and AWB records during routine registry maintenance. They need to search records, stage changes, review them, and save accurate updates without ambiguity or accidental duplication.

## Product Purpose

Maple OS provides a desktop-style internal workspace. The CCN Database app is the operational record-management surface for finding, adding, updating, exporting, and reviewing CCN/AWB data.

## Brand Personality

Dependable, unobtrusive, and precise. The interface should support repeated operational work with calm clarity rather than ask for attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard treatment, marketing-page patterns, ambiguous save states, and visual effects that make dense data work harder to scan.

## Design Principles

- Make data mutations explicit, recoverable, and difficult to repeat accidentally.
- Preserve context during search, review, and error recovery.
- Keep operational feedback specific and close to the action that caused it.
- Favor stable, scannable controls over decorative treatment.
- Treat accessibility and safety as core workflow requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide practical keyboard and screen-reader support, visible focus states, clear control labels, and reduced-motion support. No formal certification target is currently specified.
